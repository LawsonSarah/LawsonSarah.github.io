I am a PhD student at the University of Queensland, Brisbane, Australia. My primary area of interest is combinatorics. I have conducted research on hypergraph centrality, and my current PhD research focuses on the existence of Heffter arrays. I have a particular interest in relative Heffter arrays and their structure.
